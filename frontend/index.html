Paste any contract address into the scanner. Ponscan pulls on-chain data in real time from the Robinhood Chain RPC, Blockscout, rh-scan, and DexScreener, then compiles it into a full intelligence report.

What we analyze

Developer

Dev wallet, EOA/contract type, token & ETH balance, USD worth, on-chain activity, and sell history.

Funding Sources

Trace where the developer wallet received its initial funding — the origin address and transaction.

Holders

Holder count, top-10 distribution, concentration risk, and whale exposure.

Contract

Verification status, code size, implementation proxy, and Pons buy/sell fee structure.

Socials

X/Twitter handles associated with the token, plus direct links to the launchpad and DEX.

Risk Score

Weighted score (0–99) combining verification, liquidity, holder count, dev behavior, and sell activity.

Risk score methodology

The trust score starts at 55 and adjusts based on on-chain signals:

SignalImpact
Contract verified+15
Holder count > 20+10
Holder count 5–20+5
Liquidity > $5,000+10
Liquidity $1,000–$5,000+5
Dev wallet is EOA (not contract)+5
Dev holds token balance−20
Dev sell detected−25

82–99 = Low Risk · 45–81 = Medium Risk · 10–44 = High Risk

About Ponscan

A serious analytics tool for the Robinhood Chain — built for people who check before they buy.

Ponscan is a token intelligence platform. Enter any contract address and get a full breakdown: who deployed it, where the developer got their funding, what they still hold, whether they've sold, how the supply is distributed, and an overall risk score.

It's built for the Robinhood Chain ecosystem — the home of Pons launchpad projects and thousands of community tokens. No wallets are connected, no personal data is collected, and everything you see comes from public on-chain data.

Why "Scan Before You Buy"

Most losses in crypto come from buying first and researching never. Ponscan flips the order: check the developer's history, trace their funding, verify the socials, and see how concentrated the holders are — before your money ever touches the chain.

What makes Ponscan different

Dev-focused

We treat the developer as the primary risk surface — their wallet, holdings, funding, and sell behavior.

Real on-chain data

No guesswork. Every number comes from the Robinhood Chain, Blockscout, rh-scan, and DexScreener.

Fast & free

Full scans in seconds, cached for instant repeat lookups. No account, no API key, no paywall.

Disclaimer

Ponscan is an analytics tool, not financial advice. Risk scores are heuristic and may miss sophisticated patterns. Always do your own research (DYOR) before trading.
